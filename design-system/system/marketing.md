# Marketing

Posts, reels, the brochure and the case study use the same colours as the app, with Poppins for type.

## Layout
- Build pages from full-width bands that alternate `green-700` (curry leaf), white (`neutral-0`) and rice paper (`green-50`), as the brochure does.
- One big idea per band: a `mkt-kicker` label, a `mkt-headline`, a `mkt-lead` line, then the proof (a phone screen, a slip, a number).
- Turmeric is for the single call to action ("Free trial for restaurants", "Book your free trial") and highlights in headlines, never for paragraphs.
- Cards on green are white. Cards on white are rice paper. Corners `radius-2xl` to `radius-3xl`; shadows `shadow-3` only for phone mockups.

## Formats
| Format | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram post | 1080 × 1350 | headline in the top third; logo or wordmark small at the top |
| Reel or story | 1080 × 1920 | a hook in the first 2 seconds; captions in `mkt-caption` in a dark pill, centred near the bottom; end card with the coin drop and the call to action |
| Brochure | A4 portrait pages | 8 pages: cover, why switch, how it works, a day with galla, features, sample bills, FAQ, plans and contact |
| Case study | web page | the same bands; app screens in phone frames |

## Reels
- Under 25 seconds. Hook, then three to four scenes, then the end card.
- Captions appear word by word, with key words in turmeric. Most people watch muted, so the captions must tell the whole story.
- Sound: an upbeat track with sounds matched to actions (coin clink, taps, chime on payment, printer buzz, a cash-register ding for "Cash matches"). Use only music and sounds we made or have the rights to.

## Content rules
- Only show features that are built. Mark future features as "coming soon" or leave them out.
- Sample shop: "Singh Sweets", MG Road, Pune. Sample staff: Ravi (cashier). All sample figures are illustrative.
- Placeholders stay visible until filled: [@your_handle], [YOUR NUMBER].
- Hinglish lines in Roman script are welcome: "Restaurant ka hisaab, ab phone pe."
- No fake reviews, testimonials or invented customer numbers.
