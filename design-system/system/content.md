# Content and voice

galla talks like a helpful person at the counter: short, plain and calm. Owners and cashiers are busy, often reading at arm's length during a rush.

## Rules
- Use short sentences and everyday words: "bill", "table", "kitchen", "cash", "count". Avoid "transaction", "order entity", "sync".
- Use sentence case everywhere, including buttons and titles: "Send to kitchen", "Restaurant details".
- Buttons are verbs that say what happens: "Print test page", "Cancel this bill", "Open WhatsApp". Put the amount in when money moves: "Show UPI QR · ₹231".
- Speak to the person as "you"; galla never says "we" inside the app except in problem reports ("so we can fix it").
- Reassure where people worry: "Your bills are safe." "Bills are still saved on this phone."
- Errors say what went wrong and how to fix it, without blame or apology: "FSSAI numbers have 14 digits." "Only GST-registered businesses can charge GST. Add your GSTIN above."
- When the app can't decide for them, point to the right person: "Not sure which rate applies? Check with your CA."
- No exclamation marks in the product, except the printed "Thank you! Visit again." line customers expect. No emoji.

| paid out | expense voucher |
| close the day | end of day report, Z report |
| cash matches / short / extra | variance, discrepancy |
| owner, manager, cashier | admin, user, operator |

## Numbers and money
- Rupees on screen as ₹ with Indian grouping: ₹1,23,456. On paper as "Rs 231" (thermal printers can't print ₹).
- Show whole rupees on screen unless paise matter (GST lines show paise: ₹5.50).
- Times as 1:42 PM; dates as 10 Oct or Sat 10 Oct. Invoice numbers as INV-2627-000123 (financial year 2026–27).

## Hindi and Hinglish
- The app is in English for now. The Privacy Notice has a Hindi summary.
- Marketing may use Hinglish lines in Roman script: "Restaurant ka hisaab, ab phone pe." Write Hindi in Devanagari only where a proper Hindi font is available.
